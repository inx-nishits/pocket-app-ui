Guidance

Guidance for interacting with CCTV Systems to preserve evidential integrity.
What you must do
Perform and document a time check – for all cameras if applicable
Confirm the CCTV overwrite time period
Perform initial viewing at the scene to establish relevance of footage
Identify earliest recorded footage of the incident
Make contemporaneous notes of everything you do (and don’t do!) and why
Use only force-approved retrieval media
Retrieve the original/native file format
Retrieve the replay software as well as the footage
Refer to any available manuals for the CCTV system – these may be available online!
Act in accordance with your Force guidance and SOPs - if in doubt seek advice from your Digital Forensics Unit
Produce a statement to exhibit CCTV footage
Produce master and working copies of the CCTV footage
What you must not do
Don’t forget to factor in Daylight Saving if the clocks have gone forward/back around the time of the incident.
Don’t just record the screen – always retrieve the footage where possible
Don’t let a witness view CCTV footage with you at the scene
Don’t change times, dates or any settings
Don’t switch off or unplug anything without seeking advice first – only physically recover an entire CCTV system as a last resort
Don’t forget your obligations under CPIA!
Don’t put yourself at risk if a system is electrically unsafe or inaccessible
