Definition

(1) A person is in breach of this section if he:

(a) occupies a position in which he is expected to safeguard, or not to act against, the financial interests of another person,
(b) abused that position
(c) dishonestly
(d) intending by that abuse to make a gain / cause a loss

Points to Prove

  • Occupies a position of expectation to safeguard financial interests
  • Abused that position
  • Acted dishonestly
  • Intended to make a gain or cause a loss

Powers & Punishment

Recordable: Yes | Notifiable: Yes

Triable: Either way

  • Indictment: Up to 10 years' imprisonment
  • Summary: Up to 6 months' imprisonment and/or fine

Sufficient to Charge?

Anticipated Guilty Plea?

Police have authority to charge unless loss > £5000, context is exceptionally serious, or suspect is subject to Crown Court order.

Anticipated Not Guilty Plea?

CPS advice must be sought for all contested triable either way offences.

Stated Cases

R v Clark (1998)

Established that breach of trust by professionals (accountants, solicitors, etc.) usually requires immediate imprisonment unless sums are very small.

R v Kefford (2002)

Confirmed that while Clarke guidelines apply, alternative sentences may be considered for economic crimes where imprisonment is not strictly necessary for protection of public.
